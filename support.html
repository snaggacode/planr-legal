Common Questions

How do I connect with my coach?
Your coach will share an invite code with you. Enter it in the app to connect to your coach and start receiving your programs.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device to view, change, or cancel your Planr subscription.

How do I delete my account?
You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app, under your account settings. This action is irreversible.

I'm having a technical issue.
Please email us at velastudio.app@gmail.com with a description of the problem and, if possible, your device model and iOS version. This helps us resolve issues faster.

Report Content or a User

If you encounter objectionable content or behaviour within the app, you can report messages and photos, or block another user, directly in the app. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also contact us at velastudio.app@gmail.com.
